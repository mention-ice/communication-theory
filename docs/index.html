How the course works

  • Lectures

    Theory on the blackboard: definitions, proofs, examples. The lecture notes are posted here, lecture by lecture. Take your own notes.

  • TD (tutorials)

    One exam-style problem on paper, then an interactive web page where you compute and the page checks every step. Bring a laptop, a tablet or a phone. Corrections are posted here.

  • TP (labs)

    Python notebooks: you write the decoders and the receiver, on real rooms recorded with a phone. Attendance is mandatory. Solutions are posted here.

Evaluation. A 2-hour written exam, documents allowed, gives the final score. The four TPs are mandatory: your work is handed in at the end of each session and is evaluated as part of the “compétence”, not in the final score.
